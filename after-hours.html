After hours, I build tools and share what I learn.

After work I follow my interests in food, nutrition, AI and digital tools, and I turn what I learn into products and resources that other people can use.

A digital product I built

I built a free calorie checker for Nigerian and West African foods.

It lists more than 1,000 foods. For each one it shows calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates, vitamins and minerals per 100 grams, and you can enter your own portion size.

It is one of the ways I use my background in nutrition and my interest in AI and digital tools to build things other people can use.

Try the calorie checker at Wajeun.co
