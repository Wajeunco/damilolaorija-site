9-5

I lead a mental health rehabilitation service in London. My job is to keep people safe, keep the service compliant with CQC requirements, and help my team grow in their roles.

01

The work that keeps a service safe, compliant and well led.

Across a regulated care service, I am responsible for six areas.

I manage the whole staff journey.

I handle recruitment, induction, supervision, appraisals, probations and disciplinary processes.

I keep the service ready for inspection.

I run audits, spot checks and medication assessments, and I turn CQC updates into revised policy and practice.

I protect the people we support.

I manage safeguarding, care plans and risk assessments, and I apply the Mental Capacity Act and work with the wider professional team.

I lead digital change.

I introduce electronic care records and help staff adopt them.

I develop my team.

I train senior staff to become supervisors, support staff through their diplomas and deliver in-house training.

I run the practical side of the service.

I manage the rota, payroll, resources and policies, and I report findings to senior leaders.

Featured project

I moved a care service from paper records to an electronic care record system.

The problem

Medication, rotas and care records were kept on paper, which made them slow to audit and easy to lose track of.

What I did

I mapped each paper process into the new system, trained the team and worked closely with staff who were unsure about the change.

The result

Staff adopted the new system, and care records are now kept digitally instead of on paper.
